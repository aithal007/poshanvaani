# WHO Child Growth Standards data

`data/who-raw/*.txt` are copied unchanged from the World Health Organization's
`anthro` R package repository (github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/anthro,
`data-raw/growthstandards`, commit b776d8a), which is licensed under GPL-3.0.
`web/src/data/who-lms.json` is a format conversion of those tables made by
`scripts/build-who-data.mjs` and is distributed under the same GPL-3.0 terms.

Source standard: WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. WHO Child Growth
Standards: Length/height-for-age, weight-for-age, weight-for-length,
weight-for-height and body mass index-for-age. Geneva: WHO, 2006.
